# Revision 002 — focus first

## User feedback (Czech, 20 September 2026)
Keep the shared dotted 2D world and the visual quality, but remove the Blackboard corner logo, scene numbers and captions, generic slogans, Noda branding, breadcrumbs and redundant text. The viewer must always know where to look. Some graph animation/highlighting in v001 changed emphasis too abruptly. Reduce information density. Use different panel proportions, less regular timing, and let one panel disappear or turn into another in the same place. Deliver a finished MP4, not a website.

## Editorial solution
One causal journey, not a four-dashboard tour:

1. **Project card** (820×650): one project, two quiet completed rows, one active Performance row. A small camera move leads the eye to that row.
2. **Extraction**: the active row detaches and becomes a 380×240 diagnostic card. The parent card exits. This is a continuous geometric transformation in the same 2D space.
3. **Short chain**: the visible signal goes through an Optimize card (300×190) into a compact verification tile (180×180). The camera follows the signal rather than visiting equal-size screens at a fixed rhythm.
4. **Graph**: the verification tile grows into a 1270×660 Performance chart. Earlier content retires. The curve draws at constant stroke weight/opacity, with one tip following the exact same path. No animated fill, badge burst, bouncing tooltip or competing counters. The end value appears gently beside the endpoint.
5. **Resolution**: the chart retires into a compact 580×350 ready card. Its endpoint stays in world space and becomes a quiet check mark, so attention is handed over continuously.

## Attention rules
- No corner marks, footers, section numbers, outside captions, brand names, app chrome, navigation or slogans.
- Only useful UI copy: project name, task names, action/state names, chart label and result.
- One primary accent action at a time; context stays subdued and static.
- The camera and active element share a direction during a handoff.
- No back/elastic eases, bloom, flash or abrupt opacity/weight change on the graph.
- Motion-path tip and stroke progress use the same progress function.
- No 3D transforms, perspective or cuts.

## Timing (24 s; deliberately unequal beats)
0.00–1.30: establish the simple project card.
1.30–2.65: gently focus the active Performance row.
2.75–4.90: extract the row, change its proportions, retire the old card.
4.90–5.85: read the baseline score.
5.85–7.95: one signal to Optimize; one small process indicator.
7.95–9.85: follow the signal into verification.
9.95–11.95: expand the tile into the graph; clear the old context.
12.35–16.95: one continuous line draw.
16.70–18.60: gentle value reveal and reading pause.
18.65–21.40: endpoint-to-check handoff; panel shrinks to ready state.
21.40–24.00: hold the resolved result.

## Output
1920×1080, 30 fps, silent, actual Hyperframes render. Internal editable sources only; no new studio or live web preview is presented to the user. Version 001 is retained unchanged for comparison.
